Education on reporting and intake

Learn · 28 October 2026

AE Connect, a product of QORO Intelligence Inc., commits to public education on adverse-event reporting and on case intake. That work is funded with a dedicated education and awareness budget. These notes are part of the commitment: the figures and the limits on them, and a plain account of what happens when a report is never started, or is started late.

The figures are literature and regulatory sources. AE Connect did not recompute them. Spontaneous-report totals are not incidence rates. A reported outcome is not proof that a medicine caused the event. Nothing in these notes is a claim that the software changes mortality.

See the intake form

The 14-day trial is a sandbox for electronic intake, form customization, AI triage, and an E2B(R3) file. A demo is a walkthrough of the same step. The AE intake checklist lists case data to have ready before that file is prepared.
