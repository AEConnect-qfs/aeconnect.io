AE Connect’s mission

Learn · 28 October 2026

Medicines are built to save and improve lives. AE Connect’s mission is to catch adverse drug reactions before it is too late.

That sentence is the mission. It is not an estimate of deaths following from a late report. No source in Learn provides that number, so these notes do not offer one. AE Connect does not submit or transmit ICSRs. The intake draft does not make the regulatory call, and nothing here is a claim that the software changes mortality.

The cited figures, and the limits printed on them, are in Six facts, with the limits attached. Why a reaction may never be sent is in Why many reactions never become a report. The public-education commitment is in Education on reporting and intake.

See the intake form

The 14-day trial is a sandbox for electronic intake, form customization, AI triage, and an E2B(R3) file. A demo is a walkthrough of the same step. The AE intake checklist lists case data to have ready before that file is prepared.
