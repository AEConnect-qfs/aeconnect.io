What We Ask

Frequent, honest feedback. That's the deal.

We're building AE Connect alongside the teams who use it. We ask beta participants to be active collaborators, not passive users.

  • 1

    Use the platform for real AE intake

    Process actual cases through AE Connect during the beta window. We need production-representative usage to iterate meaningfully.

  • 2

    Attend monthly check-in calls

    Three 60-minute sessions, one per month, to share structured feedback, discuss workflow gaps, and review what we've built based on your input.

  • 3

    Submit feedback as you go

    Use the in-app feedback tool or contact us directly when something doesn't work as expected, or when an idea occurs to you mid-workflow.

  • 4

    Share a brief testimonial at close

    At program end, we ask for a short written or video testimonial for use on aeconnect.io. Your team's name and organization will be featured with your approval.
