What the fee covers

You paste a narrative or upload a PDF, Word file, text, email, CSV, or image. The file is parsed in the browser. The draft covers seriousness, a suggested MedDRA preferred term and system organ class, WHO-UMC causality, expectedness, a reporting timeline, and a short narrative. You accept or override every field.

The annual fee does not buy an Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, or Veeva Vault Safety license. AE Connect can feed those safety databases, or it can be the safety database for the full ICSR and the E2B(R3) file. Read the Argus, LifeSphere, and Vault Safety pages.

Questions

What is the 14-day sandbox trial?

Name, a company email, and a company name request a 14-day sandbox with unlimited cases. We set up the account. The company name becomes the Organization. It includes the electronic intake form, customization, AI triage, and E2B(R3) export. Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, Outlook, iCloud, and AOL addresses are not accepted.

Is there an annual Starter, Growth, or Scale plan?

Yes. Starter is $36,000 per year, Growth is $72,000 per year, and Scale is $120,000 per year. Those subscriptions are for companies that need a customized intake form and integration with their existing safety database. Contact sales or book a demo.

Does the subscription replace a safety database?

It can feed Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, Veeva Vault Safety, or another safety database. It can also be the safety database for the full ICSR and the E2B(R3) file. A reviewer accepts or overrides the AI draft.
