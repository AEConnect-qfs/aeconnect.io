Pricing

Two public prices. The first 5 AI-processed cases are free. Core is $20 per month after that.

Free cases

$0

First 5 AI-processed cases. No card. A case counts once when it is assessed. Opening the same case again does not spend another free case.

Core

$20/month

25 AI-processed cases included each month, then $1 per additional case. Billed through Stripe.

Those are the offers in the site JSON-LD and in the workspace. AE Connect does not publish a separate enterprise tier on this page.

What the fee covers

You paste a narrative or upload a PDF, Word file, text, email, CSV, or image. The file is parsed in the browser. The draft covers seriousness, a suggested MedDRA preferred term and system organ class, WHO-UMC causality, expectedness, a reporting timeline, and a short narrative. You accept or override every field.

The fee does not buy Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, or Veeva Vault Safety, and it does not transmit an E2B(R3) ICSR. Those safety databases remain the system of record. Read how the handoff works for Argus, LifeSphere, and Vault Safety.

Questions

What is included in the free cases?

The first 5 AI-processed cases are free, with no card. Each assessed case counts once. Re-running a case you already assessed does not use another free case.

What does Core cost?

Core is $20 per month. It includes 25 AI-processed cases in the month, then $1 per additional case. Stripe Checkout takes the card.

Is there an annual Starter, Growth, or Scale plan?

No. The public price is the free cases and Core. Older pages that listed $36,000, $72,000, or $120,000 a year are out of date.

Does the subscription replace a safety database?

No. Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, and Veeva Vault Safety remain the safety database. AE Connect is the intake and AI case-processing layer in front of them.
