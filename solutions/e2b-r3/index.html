E2B(R3) case preparation that integrates with the safety database

ICH E2B(R3) is the electronic shape of an individual case safety report. AE Connect integrates the reviewed case with any safety database. Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, and Veeva Vault Safety remain the system of record for that file.

What "E2B(R3) output" means here

The draft is organized around the concepts an E2B(R3) case needs from the source: seriousness, a MedDRA suggestion, causality, expectedness, a timeline, and a narrative. A reviewer accepts or overrides each field. AE Connect integrates that case with any safety database, including Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, and Veeva Vault Safety. Those products remain the system of record.

What to read before a submission project

The E2B(R3) checklist is a readiness list for sponsors. The ICSR submission guide separates the message standard from the product. The deadline guide is about the FDA electronic-submission expectation, which is a regulatory fact, not a feature of this workspace.
