Intake that feeds your CRO or PV partner

PV partner intake is electronic intake for a case that a contract partner will receive, or that the partner sends back. PrimeVigilance and PharmaLex are examples of pharmacovigilance service partners a sponsor might name. They are not alternatives to AE Connect, and AE Connect is not a substitute for their services.

The partner is named in the pharmacovigilance agreement. The agreement, and the safety data exchange agreement inside it or beside it, says who receives an adverse event and what has to be in the report. The case can still arrive as email. Intake is the step that turns that source into a reviewed draft before it sits in the partner's safety database, or before a case the partner processed comes back to the sponsor.

What the reviewer does

Paste the narrative or upload the file. Parsing runs in the browser. The draft covers seriousness, a suggested MedDRA term, causality, expectedness, a timeline, and a short narrative. A qualified person accepts or overrides every field. AE Connect does not decide which party owns the reporting clock, and it does not submit the ICSR.

Where the case goes

Into the safety database the agreement names. That may be Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, Veeva Vault Safety, or another database, including one a partner hosts. The collect and intake job beside IQVIA Vigilance Collect and Intake is a different page, and it is not an IQVIA Vigilance Platform alternative. The database remains the system of record.

PrimeVigilance and PharmaLex are names of their owners. AE Connect is not affiliated with them. Naming them here only says what kind of organization a pharmacovigilance agreement might point to.
