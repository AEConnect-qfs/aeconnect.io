Questions teams ask

Does AE Connect integrate the case with a safety database?

Yes. AE Connect drafts seriousness, coding, causality, expectedness, and a timeline, and a reviewer accepts or overrides the draft. AE Connect integrates that case with any safety database. Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, and Veeva Vault Safety remain the system of record for the E2B(R3) file.

How does PV case intake work?

Open the workspace, paste a narrative or choose a PDF, Word, text, email, or image file. Parsing happens in your browser. You review the draft field by field. The intake form is the case screen, not a separate patient portal.

Does it handle AE, SAE, and SUSAR cases?

You can process those narratives. The draft flags seriousness and expectedness, which a qualified person uses when deciding whether a case is an SAE or a SUSAR. The software does not make that regulatory call.

Can I use it for PQC reporting?

If a product quality complaint includes an adverse event, you can paste that narrative into the same intake. AE Connect is not a complaint-handling system or a QMS.

Does AE Connect replace Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal, or Veeva Vault Safety?

No. AE Connect integrates with any safety database, including Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, and Veeva Vault Safety. Those products remain the system of record. See Argus, LifeSphere, and Vault Safety.

Does it maintain the company core data sheet or write aggregate reports?

No. Expectedness in the draft is a suggestion to check against the current company core data sheet or Investigator's Brochure. Aggregate reports, including the IND annual report and DSUR, stay with your safety process.
