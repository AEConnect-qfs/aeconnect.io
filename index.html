Intake is the first patient-safety step.

Late or incomplete intake delays everything downstream. Reports still arrive as email, a PDF, or a forwarded message, and the case is already late, or missing the fields the clock depends on, before anyone opens a safety database.

What AE Connect is

How it works

01

Bring the source

Paste the narrative, or choose a PDF, Word file, email, text file, or image.

02

Read the draft

Seriousness, a suggested MedDRA term, causality, expectedness, and a reporting clock. AI drafts only. Each field has a short reason.

03

Review and file

A qualified person accepts or overrides every field. File the case in the safety database you already run, or keep the ICSR and the E2B(R3) file in AE Connect.

The longer intake note is on the product page. A walkthrough is the demo.

Human sign-off, and the limits

  • AI drafts only. A qualified person accepts or overrides every field.
  • Not a certified connector. AE Connect does not submit or transmit ICSRs. Product names belong to their owners.
  • A product quality complaint is in scope only when the complaint describes an adverse event. This is not a QMS.
  • No DSUR, no IND annual report, and no other aggregate reports.
