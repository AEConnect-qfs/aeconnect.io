Design partners

Design partners are drug-safety teams who will use the intake workspace with their own safety database and tell us what the integration still gets wrong. AE Connect integrates with any safety database. Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, and Veeva Vault Safety remain the system of record.

This page does not list customers, quotes, or results. There is no published outcome to infer. If we work together, the safety database you already run stays the system of record.

What we are looking for

  • A named person who reviews adverse-event narratives.
  • A safety database already in use, or a clear plan for which one will hold the official case. Typical names are Oracle Argus, ArisGlobal LifeSphere, and Veeva Vault Safety.
  • Willingness to say which fields in the draft were useful and which ones a reviewer had to override.

Questions

Do design partners replace our safety database?
