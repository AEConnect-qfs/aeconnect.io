What Oracle says Argus is for

Oracle's Argus Safety documentation describes Argus as the place to enter and intake adverse event cases, code medical and product terms, perform medical and quality assessments, and generate expedited and periodic reports. The Argus Cloud subscription page describes Argus Interchange as the component that transmits E2B reports to agencies and partners. Whether your subscription includes every module is a question for your contract, not for this page.

That is a system of record. It is the right home for the official case when a partner agreement, a CRO model, or the volume of expedited and periodic reporting needs one. AE Connect should not be purchased instead of that.

What AE Connect adds with Argus

Source documents still arrive as email and PDFs. The workspace takes a pasted narrative or an upload. Parsing happens in the browser. The draft covers seriousness, a suggested MedDRA preferred term and system organ class, WHO-UMC causality, expectedness, a reporting timeline, and a short narrative. A person accepts or overrides every field.

AE Connect integrates the reviewed case with Argus and with any other safety database. Argus remains the system of record. The Argus comparison table puts those jobs side by side. A company email requests a 14-day sandbox. Start a 14-day trial. That trial is not a bid against an Argus license.

Questions

Is AE Connect an Oracle Argus alternative?

No. Argus remains the safety database and the system of record. AE Connect integrates with Argus and with any other safety database.

Can AE Connect send cases into Argus?

Yes. After a reviewer accepts the draft, AE Connect integrates the case with Argus and with any other safety database. Argus remains the system of record. AE Connect is not an Argus replacement.

When should a team run Argus?

When the pharmacovigilance plan needs a system of record with electronic submission and periodic reporting. Many early-phase teams already have that requirement because a partner named it.

Does this page claim Argus-style validation?

No. This site does not claim GAMP 5, 21 CFR Part 11, or a customer validation package.
