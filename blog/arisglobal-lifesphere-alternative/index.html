Looking for an ArisGlobal LifeSphere alternative

QORO Team · 23 October 2026 · about 6 minutes

People search for an ArisGlobal LifeSphere alternative when the safety database they were told to buy does not match the team they have. This page is a factual comparison, not a feature matrix and not a claim that ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety is a poor product. ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety is built for organizations that process individual cases as a system of record, submit them, and support periodic reporting with a drug-safety staff. AE Connect is a case-intake workspace. It is not ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety, it does not connect to it, and it is not a certified alternative.

What ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety is for

ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety is ArisGlobal's cloud platform for pharmacovigilance case management, used by sponsors and service providers that want a single safety system for case processing and downstream regulatory work. Older contracts, training decks, and some staff still say Safety One when they mean the ArisGlobal safety database. This page uses LifeSphere Safety for the current platform and treats Safety One as that earlier name, not as a second product we are scoring. If your contract uses a different ArisGlobal module name, use the contract, not this page.

When it is the right system

Buy or keep ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety, or a CRO that already runs it, when the program needs a system of record for ICSRs. Typical signs: you are past a handful of cases, a partner's pharmacovigilance agreement names a database, you are responsible for expedited submission in more than one region, or you are about to produce real aggregate reports from a validated case series. In those situations an "alternative" that cannot submit and cannot hold the official case is the wrong purchase. AE Connect should not be that purchase.

When the search is really about weight

Early-phase biotechs often have more database than events. A medical monitor, a part-time pharmacovigilance lead, and a CRO mailbox are the whole function. The cases that matter still arrive as email and PDFs. The database login is something the vendor trained six months ago. The mismatch is described without the product-by-product framing in Argus, Safety One, and Veeva for early-phase teams and in the drug safety inbox note.

In that setting, "ArisGlobal LifeSphere alternative" usually means one of three things: a lighter place to structure the cases you do have, a front door beside ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety so the inbox stops being the record, or a delay while you decide whether you need the database this year. Only the first two are something AE Connect can be, and only as intake and review.

Safety One and LifeSphere, without a fake matrix

Vendor names move faster than SOPs. A pharmacovigilance plan that says Safety One and a vendor that says LifeSphere may be describing one system of record across a rename, or they may be describing a specific module you do not have. Confirm that with ArisGlobal or with the partner who holds the environment. AE Connect is not a party to that environment either way.

LifeSphere, like Argus and Vault Safety, assumes configured workflows, trained users, and a case volume that justifies both. A two-person clinical team with an occasional SUSAR does not become a LifeSphere customer by wanting organized intake. They become one when the system of record has to submit and has to survive an audit of the official case.

What AE Connect is, in this comparison

The AE Connect workspace takes a pasted narrative or an uploaded PDF, Word file, text, email, CSV, or image. Parsing happens in the browser. The draft covers seriousness, a suggested MedDRA preferred term and system organ class, WHO-UMC causality, expectedness, a reporting timeline, and a short narrative. A person accepts or overrides every field. The first 5 cases are free. Core is $20 per month for 25 AI-processed cases, then $1 each.

It does not create user accounts inside ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety. It does not map fields into ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety. It does not submit E2B(R3) and it does not write a PSUR, PBRER, or DSUR. If ArisGlobal LifeSphere Safety is the system of record, the official case is entered there by a person after the draft has been reviewed. Calling that an integration would be false.

The same boundary applies to the other two databases. Read the Argus, LifeSphere, and Vault Safety notes as a set, and use AI case processing for what the draft itself contains.

FAQ

Is AE Connect an ArisGlobal LifeSphere alternative?

It is an alternative only for intake and a reviewed draft, not for LifeSphere Safety as a system of record. AE Connect does not process cases inside LifeSphere, does not replace Safety One, and does not submit ICSRs.

Does AE Connect integrate with LifeSphere or Safety One?

No. There is no connector. Working alongside means a person can use the workspace to understand a source document and then enter the official case in the ArisGlobal system if that system is the record.

Which name should our SOP use, LifeSphere or Safety One?

The name on the contract and on the environment you actually log into. This page cannot see that. QORO is not ArisGlobal and does not administer those tenants.

Can LifeSphere's automation and AE Connect be stacked?

Not as a supported integration. If a vendor proposes an automated handoff, treat it as a project that does not exist today. The workspace has no LifeSphere API in this product.
