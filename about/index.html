About AE Connect

Built by PV practitioners, for PV practitioners

AE Connect is a product of QORO Intelligence Inc. — a quality and pharmacovigilance technology company founded by industry veterans who spent years building GxP infrastructure at biotech companies and saw the same gap everywhere.

The problem we're solving

Small and mid-size IND sponsors are caught between two bad options: sprawling enterprise safety databases built for large MAHs, or ad-hoc spreadsheet and email processes that don't scale and can't pass an FDA inspection.

AE Connect is the intake layer that sits in between — purpose-built for teams running one to ten studies, with AI-assisted triage, a validated case workflow, and E2B(R3)-compliant output. Right-sized for where you are, ready for where you're going.

Why we built it

Our founders have collectively spent 15+ years building pharmacovigilance programs at Gilead Sciences, Prothena Biosciences, Kura Oncology, and Geron Corporation. Every engagement involved the same manual, error-prone AE intake process.

We built AE Connect to solve the problem we lived.
