AE Connect works with Veeva Vault Safety

AE Connect is not a Veeva Vault Safety alternative. Vault Safety remains the system of record. AE Connect is electronic intake for Veeva Vault Safety: AE and ICSR intake in front of the database.

Veeva documents Web Intake Forms that a respondent can submit without opening Vault. Vault creates an Inbox Item from that submission, from email, or from a file. A user reviews the Inbox Item and can promote it to a Case. AE Connect is not a Web Intake Form and it is not the Inbox Item. It is the reviewer workspace for a source already in hand. After the reviewer accepts the draft, the case can be integrated with Vault Safety. Vault Safety remains the system of record.
