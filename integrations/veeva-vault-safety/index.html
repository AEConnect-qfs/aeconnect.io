AE Connect works with Veeva Vault Safety

AE Connect is not a Veeva Vault Safety alternative. Vault Safety remains the safety database. AE Connect is the intake and AI case-processing layer in front of it.

What Veeva's Vault Help says Vault Safety does, and what AE Connect actually does.
JobSafety databaseWhat AE Connect adds in front
RoleSafety database. Veeva's Safety Suite overview describes Vault Safety as end-to-end management of intake, medical review, case processing, and submission, plus aggregate reporting.Intake and a reviewed draft before that case is booked in Vault Safety.
System of recordTypically Vault Safety, when the plan names it. Veeva describes it as a single source of truth for safety data on Vault.No.
APIsThe same Veeva page says Vault Safety has open APIs and technology integrations. That is Veeva's platform, configured by the customer.AE Connect does not call the Vault Safety API and does not write inbox items or cases into Vault.
AI extraction and triageAny automation inside a customer's Vault is their configuration. This page does not describe it.Seriousness, expectedness, WHO-UMC causality, and a reporting timeline for a person to accept or override.
MedDRA coding assistCoding in Vault uses the customer's MedDRA license.A suggested preferred term and system organ class.
NarrativeMedical review in Vault Safety is where Veeva says the case is processed.A short draft narrative to take into that review.
E2B(R3) outputVeeva describes submission of adverse events as part of Vault Safety. The gateway and the message are configured there.AE Connect does not transmit E2B(R3). The reviewed draft is the package a processor uses when they enter the case.
Aggregate reportsVeeva's overview lists aggregate reporting in the Safety suite. SafetyDocs is a related content application. Generating tables still requires the Safety license, according to Veeva's SafetyDocs notes.AE Connect does not write PSUR, PBRER, DSUR, or an IND annual report.

Source: Veeva Vault Help, Veeva Safety Suite Overview. Veeva can change that page.

There is no certified connector that writes the case into the safety database for you. A reviewer accepts or overrides the draft in AE Connect. The official case is then booked in the safety database by a person. Calling a silent API an integration would be false. The integration is the workflow: intake and AI case processing here, system of record there.
