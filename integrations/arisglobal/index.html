LifeSphere ICSR intake that works with ArisGlobal

AE Connect works with ArisGlobal LifeSphere. It is not an ArisGlobal alternative. LifeSphere Safety remains the safety database. Teams sometimes still say Safety One for an ArisGlobal safety system. Oracle also uses Safety One in the name Safety One Argus, which is a different product. This page is about ArisGlobal.

ArisGlobal LifeSphere integration here is electronic intake for ArisGlobal: LifeSphere AE intake and LifeSphere ICSR intake in front of that database. ArisGlobal lists its own intake modules, including Advanced Intake, beside case management. AE Connect is not Advanced Intake and not MultiVigilance. It is a separate workspace that integrates the reviewed case with LifeSphere. LifeSphere remains the system of record.
