Electronic AE and ICSR intake that works with Oracle Argus

AE Connect works with Oracle Argus. It is not an Oracle Argus alternative. Argus remains the system of record. This is electronic intake for Oracle Argus: Argus AE intake and Argus ICSR intake in front of the database, including Safety One Argus.

Safety One Argus intake and Consolidated Intake

Oracle documents Consolidated Intake as part of a Safety One Argus subscription. Safety One Argus intake ingests adverse-event documents by email, API, EDI, or upload into an intake worklist. The Argus intake form, including the Argus reporter form, is Oracle's case form. A subscription may or may not include the Safety One Intake add-on. AE Connect is not Consolidated Intake, not that add-on, and not the Argus reporter form. It is a separate workspace: paste or upload a source the reviewer already has, accept or override the draft, and feed cases into Argus.

Questions

Is AE Connect an Oracle Argus alternative?

No. Argus remains the safety database and the system of record. AE Connect integrates with it for intake, AI case processing, MedDRA coding assist, and narrative drafting, and it integrates with any other safety database.

Does AE Connect integrate with Argus?

Yes. Oracle Argus integration here means the reviewer accepts the draft and AE Connect can feed cases into Argus. Argus remains the system of record. This is not an Argus alternative.

Does AE Connect submit the E2B(R3) file?

AE Connect integrates the case with Argus and with any other safety database. Argus remains the system of record for the E2B(R3) file.
